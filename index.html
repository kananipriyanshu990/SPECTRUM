Club Introduction

Department of Electronics & Robotics

SPECTRUM is the flagship hardware engineering club of dharmsinh Desai University. Formed by student innovators, makers, and researchers, the club acts as an open collaborative laboratory where theoretical classroom concepts are transformed into physical, testable machines.

We maintain an inventory of development boards, rapid prototyping equipment, oscilloscopes, and testing benches. Beyond individual projects, our culture emphasizes deep peer mentorship—senior members train incoming batches in schematic creation, microarchitecture firmware, and autonomous ROS navigation stacks.

Research & Development

Core Technical Domains

Our members work across four specialized fields, bridging the gap between low-level silicon, intelligent algorithms, and physical actuation.

Embedded Systems

Bare-metal development, RTOS implementation, firmware engineering, and low-level communication protocols across ARM Cortex-M, STM32, and ESP32 platforms.

FreeRTOS • Bare-Metal C • CAN/I2C

Robotics

Autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), forward & inverse kinematics, 2D/3D LiDAR SLAM, path planning using ROS 2, and physical simulation in Gazebo.

ROS 2 • SLAM • Kinematics

VLSI

Digital design and hardware description languages (Verilog/VHDL), FPGA synthesis on Xilinx Vivado, timing analysis, and ASIC design workflow fundamentals.

Verilog • FPGA • Vivado

AI / ML

Computer vision pipelines, TinyML for microcontrollers, neural network quantization, and edge acceleration running on NVIDIA Jetson architectures.

Edge AI • TinyML • OpenCV
